# Cross-Rail Governance

## Rule

Quellwert/Room16 and Steuer/Kanzlei Rollforward are independent product rails. A safe improvement in one rail is not evidence of readiness in the other.

`Vega` is not a Steuer product component. Vega/Codex is only the external project/coding/governance assistant role used to implement, verify, document, and route work. The Steuer/Kanzlei Rollforward product itself remains local, deterministic, Excel-first, and without AI/LLM runtime or tax-decision logic.

## Allowed Reuse

- Process discipline.
- Local manifests and checksums.
- Verifier structure.
- Audit bundles.
- Operator review checklists.
- Read-only-first intake.
- Human-governed gates.

## Forbidden Reuse

- Financial-research claims as tax logic.
- Tax workbook/fixture status as Quellwert launch readiness.
- Room16 outcome monitoring as Steuer quality evidence.
- KAP mapping success as Quellwert compliance evidence.
- Payment/production/public readiness from local verifier success alone.
- Vega/Codex governance activity as a Steuer runtime capability.

## Routing

- If a task mentions Quellwert, Room16, LIONCOM, public research, Founding Circle, sample analyses, outcome monitoring, or revenue launch: route to the Quellwert rail.
- If a task mentions Steuer, Kanzlei, Rollforward, Excel, KAP, DATEV, ERL_2024.xlsx, workbook integrity, or fixture intake: route to the Steuer rail.
- If a task mentions both: stop and split the work before implementation.
