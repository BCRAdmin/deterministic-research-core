## Strategic Decision

Quellwert should not wait until Room16 is a perfect analyst machine. The launchable product is a trusted, non-advice research surface with a paid founder cohort behind it. Room16 remains the internal evidence engine; Quellwert becomes the public and revenue-facing editorial layer.

## Revenue Thesis

Fastest credible money path:

1. Launch a public trust surface with methodology, archive, and a few safe sample analyses.
2. Sell a limited founding cohort before building a heavy membership platform.
3. Keep paid output as evidence-backed company research and decision context, not personalised investment advice.
4. Use custom research/dossier work as the fastest cash bridge while membership proof develops.

## Three Launch Lanes

### Lane A - Public Trust Surface

Goal: make Quellwert visible and trustworthy.

- Publish landing page, methodology, archive, legal pages, and sample analysis pages.
- Keep public copy free of `Buy`, `Sell`, price targets, promised returns, portfolio instructions, and personalised advice.
- Show process quality: source status, evidence method, non-advice note, analyst uncertainty, publication gate.
- Use `GOOGL`, `SNOW`, `MSFT` as safer public examples only if current gates remain green.

### Lane B - Paid Founder Cohort

Goal: first recurring revenue with controlled scope.

- Offer: `Quellwert Founding Circle`.
- Suggested first package: weekly research brief, company watchlist notes, methodology notes, and monthly live/async Q&A.
- Suggested price test: `19-49 EUR/month` for readers, `99-199 EUR/month` for pro research-ops access, and `499-1500 EUR` one-off custom dossier work.
- Start manual: Stripe/payment link or invoice, email/Notion/locked page delivery, no complex member app until demand is proven.
- Limit first cohort to `10-25` users to protect quality and learn fast.

### Lane C - Room16 Evidence Engine

Goal: keep the machine useful without pretending it is public-ready automation.

- Continue outcome tracking: `10D` on `2026-06-01`, then `20D`, then `60D`.
- Promote only human-reviewed, public-gated material into Quellwert.
- Use 5D findings as monitoring signals only: `AVGO` false-pass candidate, `NOW/RKLB/ZS` curated false-block candidates, `MDB` positive monitor.
- Do not relax guards, ratings, calibration, reports, or public output from 1D/5D alone.

## Compliance And Safety Gates

- Before charging for anything that could be an investment recommendation, run a dedicated compliance review.
- BaFin states that investment and investment strategy recommendations require objectivity, care, identity, conflict disclosures, and notification obligations under WpHG sections 85/86.
- ESMA warns that investment recommendations, including social media-style recommendations, must let readers distinguish facts from opinions and require objective, transparent presentation under the Market Abuse Regulation framework.
- Until reviewed: no personalised advice, no model portfolio, no price targets, no "buy/sell now", no guaranteed returns, no undisclosed positions/conflicts.

Sources:

- BaFin: https://www.bafin.de/EN/Aufsicht/BoersenMaerkte/Marktmissbrauch/Anlagestrategie/anlagestrategie_node_en.html
